Inventory Management System

A full-stack inventory, sales and analytics platform designed to centralize product management, customer records, sales processing and inventory reporting.

Python Flask Firebase Firestore HTML CSS JavaScript Bootstrap Chart.js OpenPyXL

THE PROBLEM

Businesses often struggle with fragmented inventory operations, disconnected product records, and inconsistent sales visibility, which leads to inaccurate stock tracking, delayed decision-making, and inefficient operational workflows.

THE SOLUTION

InventoryPro centralizes product management, stock monitoring, sales tracking, customer records, and reporting into a unified system. It streamlines operational workflows, improves data visibility, supports low-stock alerts, and enables structured reporting for better business control.
